Quick context. CoAd sells a PEO (payroll, HR, benefits, workers' comp and compliance in one) and stand-alone payroll (CoAd Pay, formerly PrimePay) to small and mid-size businesses with employees. The ads are there to bring in sales-qualified leads from owners and decision makers, and a PEO client is worth far more than payroll alone. Someone logging in to an HR portal, looking for a job, filing a claim or taking a training course isn't a buyer.
