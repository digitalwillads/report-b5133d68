Search terms audit, Oct 18 to Nov 16

We cut about $51,608 a month of wasted search spend

CoAd spent $99,670 on search over the last 30 days (the part Google lets us see), and only about 20% of it reached people who could actually buy. Most of the rest went to people looking for a login page, a job or a free form. We shut off the three biggest leaks on Nov 17.

Problem

38% of the money went to people trying to log in to their payroll

These are employees at other companies looking for their own payroll or benefits site, like Dayforce, bswift or Employee Navigator. They see a CoAd ad, click it, and CoAd pays for the click. None of them are ever going to buy a PEO.

Fixed Nov 17

We blocked login searches on all 10 non-brand campaigns

We added 70 negative keywords. CoAd's ads now stay off any search with a login or website word in it (login, sign in, portal, .com) or the name of a portal only employees use, like Dayforce, bswift, Alight or UltiPro.
